As a final project for Mechatronics, my team designed and built an autonomous robot for a competitive robotics tournament. The objective was to collect and control more cubes than the opposing robot within a timed match. Our robot performed extremely well throughout the competition and made it into the final single-elimination bracket.

I was heavily involved in the robot’s mechanical, electrical, and software development. I was responsible for designing the robot’s arm and gear system in CAD and determined appropriate gear ratios by analyzing the torque and current limits of the available motors. The gears were designed and toleranced specifically for reliable 3D printing and assembly.

I also handled the wiring and integration of the Arduino, motors, H-bridges, servos, and color sensor, and programmed the robot in C for autonomous operation. In addition to assembly and testing, I worked through mechanical reliability issues during development, including vibrations causing fasteners to loosen, and refined the design through iterative debugging and testing before competition.

Throughout the project, I also was in charge of managing material and printing costs to keep the design within budget while making efficient use of available resources.
